There are plenty of places to post transport photos, but most of them are either too noisy or too dead. Spotted sits somewhere in the middle — a feed of transport shots from people who actually care what model that bus is.

No algorithms, no ads, no engagement bait. Just a timeline of your latest finds and a way to see what other people have spotted nearby.
