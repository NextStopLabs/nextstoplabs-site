My Transport Times
FORMERLY MYBUS TIMES

A bus operations sandbox and simulation. Build routes, manage fleets, create timetables. When you start a company, choose between sandbox or economy mode with easy, medium, or hard difficulty - then try not to let it fall apart.

We basically built this because we wanted a version of bustimes.org where we could do whatever we wanted. It's "fantasy football" but for transport.

There isn't really a "right" way to use MTT. Some of us use it to keep a list of our model buses, some use it to track stuff for other games, and some just play it as a standalone thing. When you start a company, you pick your difficulty - that's it.

Everything lives in one place now. Draw routes, manage your fleet, create timetables - and if you opted into the economy, you'd better keep an eye on the bank balance. Easy, medium, or hard. Your call.

Sandbox

No money, no pressure. Draw routes, list your fleet, make timetables - it's just for building your world and showing it off. There's no way to lose.

Economy

Pick easy, medium, or hard. Manage your bank balance, buy and sell buses, and try to run a company without going bust. The difficulty you choose changes how tight the margins are.
