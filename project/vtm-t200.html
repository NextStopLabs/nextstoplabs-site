It's based on the Ticketer T200 — the ticket machines you see on buses. Rough port from the MBT V1 era, so it's janky. Don't expect polish.

Your MTT companies sync in. You pick a bus, then a route, then the ticket page lets you issue whatever tickets you set up in MTT. You can change the stop, adjust passenger count, and mess with basic functions. Waybills log what you've issued.

That's it. It's jank.

Select Bus → Route → Tickets

Pick your bus, pick your route, then issue tickets. The workflow is basic — choose a vehicle from your MTT fleet, pick a route, and you're on the ticket screen.

Ticket Issuing

Issue whatever tickets you set up in MTT. Change the stop, adjust passenger alignment, and mess with the basic functions. It's a Ticketer-style interface, just jankier.

MTT Sync

Your companies, vehicles, routes, and tickets pull straight from My Transport Times. If it's in MTT, it shows up here.
